# Clip Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026. Clip Desk is the private publishing tool of Hard Truths | Podcast Clips (@hardtruthspodcastclips).

## What Clip Desk is

Clip Desk is a single-user tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It uploads, schedules and measures short video clips on the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

## Data it uses

- Google / YouTube (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API): permission to upload and update videos on the owner's channel and to read that channel's video statistics.
- Meta / Instagram (Instagram Graph API): permission to publish reels to the owner's Instagram professional account, read its insights, and use the Facebook Page linked to it.
- Sign-in tokens, kept in a file readable only by the owner on the owner's computer.

## What it doesn't do

- It collects no data about viewers or other people.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
- It sends data only to Google and Meta, to carry out the owner's own posts and reports.

Clip Desk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Facebook settings under Business Integrations (Meta), and can delete the local token file.
